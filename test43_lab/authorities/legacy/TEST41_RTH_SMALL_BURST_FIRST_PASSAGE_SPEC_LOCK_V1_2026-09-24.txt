On signal bar t:

1\. impulse bar t-2:

   Close\[t-2\] \> Open\[t-2\]

   AND Close\[t-2\] \> max(High\[t-5\], High\[t-4\], High\[t-3\]).

2\. pullback bar t-1:

   Close\[t-1\] \< Open\[t-1\]

   AND Low\[t-1\] \>= (High\[t-2\] + Low\[t-2\]) / 2.

   Close\[t\] \> Open\[t\]

3\. t bearish and Close\[t\] \< Low\[t-1\].

bar t close.

\#\#\# M3 — CONFIRMED MICRO STRUCTURE TRANSITION BREAK



Purpose:

mechanically translate Trader Tom-style structure break without discretionary swing selection.



Use confirmed 3-bar pivots only.



A pivot high at bar j becomes known only after bar j+1 completes:

High\[j\] \> High\[j-1\]

AND High\[j\] \>= High\[j+1\].



A pivot low at bar j becomes known only after bar j+1 completes:

Low\[j\] \< Low\[j-1\]

AND Low\[j\] \<= Low\[j+1\].



Maintain the two most recent confirmed pivot highs and two most recent confirmed pivot lows.



LONG setup:

\- bearish microstructure exists:

  most recent confirmed pivot high \< previous confirmed pivot high

  AND

  most recent confirmed pivot low \< previous confirmed pivot low.

\- current completed signal bar closes at least one market tick ABOVE the most recent confirmed pivot high.

\- current signal bar is bullish.



SHORT:

exact inverse:

\- most recent pivot high \> previous pivot high

\- most recent pivot low \> previous pivot low

\- current completed bar closes at least one tick BELOW the most recent confirmed pivot low

\- current signal bar bearish.



No hindsight swing relabeling.

Once a pivot is confirmed, later bars cannot move that pivot backward.



Signal:

structure-break bar close.
