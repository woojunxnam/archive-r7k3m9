WORKSTREAM 5 — FEAR / PANIC + BULL-PULLBACK NEURON RESEARCH

\============================================================

  

Goal:

Test whether temporal market-state transitions add information beyond V1/G0/MATH-A, and only then test whether a biologically inspired spiking system adds value beyond simpler models.

  

Do NOT begin by constructing a 2,000-5,000 neuron trader.

  

Stage N0 — Phenomenon falsification first.

  

N0-A Panic-Reversal hypothesis:

FEAR -\> PANIC -\> SELLING EXHAUSTION -\> RELIEF -\> RECOVERY.

  

Test whether high fear with panic decelerating/exhaustion/recovery has better forward return, lower MAE and higher first-passage success than equally high fear that is still worsening.

  

N0-B Bull-Pullback hypothesis:

BULL TREND -\> IMPULSE -\> HEALTHY PULLBACK -\> PULLBACK EXHAUSTION -\> REACCELERATION -\> CONTINUATION.

  

Test whether, inside an already-established bullish trend, pullback exhaustion plus bullish reacceleration predicts:

\- better forward return;

\- lower MAE;

\- higher probability of new trend high before trend failure;

\- better rebound/continuation probability than pullback alone.

  

SOXL and TQQQ must be analyzed independently first.

Cluster overlapping bars into episodes; do not treat every bar within one selloff/pullback as independent.

  

Mandatory baselines before SNN:

\- current G0 mechanism;

\- simple deterministic transition rule;

\- logistic regression;

\- gradient boosting;

\- HMM/state model;

\- a small recurrent/reservoir or GRU-style baseline where justified.

  

Only if temporal-state evidence survives should an SNN be built.

  

Initial neural task should be narrow:

ALLOW ENTRY vs WAIT.

Do NOT initially ask the neural system to learn the full BUY/ADD/HOLD/REDUCE/EXIT portfolio policy. V1 continues to own inventory/risk mechanics.

  

Initial SNN should be small and falsifiable (roughly hundreds to \~1,000 LIF neurons rather than automatically 5,000). Compare fixed/random weights, disabled recurrence/plasticity and multiple random seeds. Move to reward-modulated STDP only if simpler recurrent/SNN models establish incremental value.

  

Cross-asset generalization tests:

\- train SOXL -\> freeze -\> test TQQQ;

\- train TQQQ -\> freeze -\> test SOXL;

\- normalized inputs only;

\- no ticker-specific retuning in transfer tests.

  

Key outputs:

\- panic worsening vs panic exhausting outcome distributions;

\- bull pullback worsening vs exhaustion vs reacceleration outcomes;

\- false-bottom avoidance;

\- WAIT value;

\- first-passage probability;

\- MAE/MFE and recovery time;

\- trend-survival and new-high-before-failure probability;

\- performance versus G0/MATH-A/simple ML;

\- seed dispersion and ablations.

  

If Phase N0 finds no stable phenomenon, STOP / REFORMULATE and do not build the SNN.

  

\============================================================

WORKSTREAM 6 — DISTINCTIVE INTRADAY TRADER / EXECUTION INTUITION

\============================================================

Goal:

If Workstream 5 establishes a real temporal-state edge, evolve the artificial trader into a distinctive intraday decision agent that can repeatedly identify meaningful short-horizon market "wrinkles" during RTH without being forced to trade noise.

  

"Meaningful wrinkle" means a causal, repeatable intraday structure with measurable expected value after costs, such as:

\- panic acceleration -\> exhaustion -\> relief;

\- bull impulse -\> pullback -\> exhaustion -\> reacceleration;

\- failed breakdown -\> reclaim;

\- local trend continuation after a controlled pullback;

\- overextension -\> distribution / exhaustion for reduction.

Do NOT define every small price wiggle as a tradeable wrinkle. Multiple trades per day is an aspiration, not a quota. If valid opportunities are absent, WAIT is correct.

  

Trader architecture:

Use two interacting time scales.

SLOW CONTEXT BRAIN: daily/60m/15m trend strength, regime, volatility, relative SOXL/TQQQ state, inventory/exposure and persistent memory.

FAST REFLEX BRAIN: 1m/3m/5m velocity, acceleration, wick/CLV, failed lows/highs, rebound/reacceleration, fear/panic/relief/euphoria transitions and time-of-day.

The trader's persistent latent state is its operational "intuition"; this is learned state/memory, not consciousness.

  

Execution intuition becomes a separate decision layer after an opportunity is approved. Candidate actions eventually include:

\- WAIT;

\- MARKET BUY / MARKET SELL when opportunity decay and urgency dominate expected execution cost;

\- PASSIVE LIMIT BUY / LIMIT SELL when edge is expected to persist and price improvement is worth non-fill risk;

\- MARKETABLE LIMIT as a bounded-urgency compromise;

\- CANCEL / REPLACE / LET EXPIRE when the state changes;

\- HOLD / REDUCE / EXIT for position management.

  

The order-type decision must maximize expected NET execution value, not merely fill rate. Conceptually compare:

EV\_market = expected strategy edge captured now - spread/slippage/fees.

EV\_limit = fill\_probability \* expected edge if filled + nonfill\_value - adverse\_selection - fees.

Choose MARKET only when the value of immediate participation exceeds the expected price-improvement/non-fill tradeoff. Choose LIMIT only when waiting does not destroy the underlying edge.

  

Research constraints:

\- Do not give the neural model unrestricted order discretion until ALLOW-vs-WAIT and temporal-state alpha are validated.

\- First compare deterministic execution rules against learned execution choice.

\- With OHLCV-only historical data, market/limit backtests are proxies. Use conservative bar-ordering assumptions and do not claim queue position, exact spread capture or certified partial-fill probabilities.

\- If higher-frequency quote/BBO data later becomes available, use it only as a separate execution-research authority.

\- In live production, broker truth and actual fills remain authoritative; the trader may propose order type/price but the Portfolio/Risk Governor can veto or cap it.

\- No action may bypass position, margin, loss, exposure or kill-switch limits.

  

Intraday-master research sequence after N0/N1 success:

IM0. Event atlas: catalog all statistically meaningful panic/pullback/reacceleration/failed-break/reversal wrinkles by time of day and regime.

IM1. Opportunity detector: predict WAIT vs actionable wrinkle and estimate direction, horizon, first-passage probability, MAE/MFE and expected opportunity lifetime.

IM2. Execution selector: frozen comparison of MARKET vs LIMIT vs bounded marketable-limit using the same opportunity set; optimize net capture, not gross fill rate.

IM3. Position manager: test HARVEST vs HOLD-TREND vs REDUCE based on continuation/failure probabilities while V1 risk mechanics remain authoritative.

IM4. Distinctive trader memory: add episodic memory, pain/reward memory, uncertainty and exposure satiety; verify that intuition is stable across seeds and periods.

IM5. Cross-asset transfer: SOXL -\> frozen TQQQ and TQQQ -\> frozen SOXL; later allow the trader to choose which instrument has the better current opportunity under a shared capital governor.

IM6. Intraday portfolio test: measure whether valid wrinkles provide multiple independent opportunities on active days, improve weekly median/flat-week rate and return/capital-time, without materially worsening DD/tail risk.

  

Required metrics beyond PnL:

\- actionable opportunities/day distribution (not forced minimum);

\- accepted vs rejected wrinkle count;

\- expected and realized opportunity lifetime;

\- market-vs-limit chosen count;

\- fill/no-fill proxy and opportunity cost of missed fills;

\- implementation shortfall / modeled slippage;

\- edge captured vs theoretical edge available;

\- MAE/MFE and time underwater;

\- trade overlap/correlation so repeated trades are not counted as independent alpha;

\- PnL and DD by wrinkle type, regime and time-of-day;

\- performance after transaction-cost and execution-delay stress;

\- seed/model dispersion where learned models are used.

  

Success is NOT defined as "trades every day." Success means the trader learns when a short-lived intraday structure is worth acting on, when to wait, and whether immediacy (market) or price improvement (limit) has higher expected net value. Only after this survives simpler baselines and chronological validation should the distinctive trader receive broader live decision authority.

  

\============================================================

MULTI-TIMESCALE MEMORY + HOLDING-HORIZON REQUIREMENT

  

The distinctive neuron trader must NOT be limited to the current RTH session as its information context. At every decision it may use all causally available prior history, including the current session, previous completed RTH session(s), multiple prior days, and prior-week context. Construct explicit memory horizons rather than repeatedly feeding only the current-day tape.

  

Recommended causal context stack:

\- micro: last 1-15 minutes;

\- intraday: last 15-120 minutes;

\- session: current RTH structure from 09:30 to now;

\- prior-session: previous RTH OHLCV/path, close location, trend, volatility, volume, drawdown/recovery and ending neural state;

\- multi-day: rolling 2-5 completed sessions;

\- swing: approximately 1-4 completed weeks where useful for trend/regime/context.

  

Locally derive multi-resolution observations from the canonical data: 1m, 3m, 5m, 15m, 30m, 60/65m, daily, and rolling multi-day/week features. Do not reset the trader's learned/internal state to neutral at 16:00 merely because the RTH session ends. Preserve appropriately decayed memory across sessions.

  

At the next RTH open, provide the known overnight gap from the prior RTH close. If reliable premarket/after-hours data are later added to the validated data authority, they may become a separate sensory channel; do not silently mix unvalidated extended-hours data into the canonical RTH model.

  

The trader's holding horizon is also adaptive. Do NOT force every position flat at the close. A position may be:

\- intraday only;

\- held overnight;

\- held for several sessions;

\- retained longer while a validated trend-continuation state remains active.

  

The neuron/trader should make an explicit HOLD-HORIZON decision from causal state, for example:

INTRADAY HARVEST / HOLD INTO CLOSE / HOLD OVERNIGHT / SWING HOLD / REDUCE / EXIT.

  

Research the continuation decision separately from entry. At each potential close/exit boundary compare the expected value of exiting now versus holding through the next horizon, including overnight gap/tail risk, capital-time, exposure, transaction cost, and drawdown consequences. Never reward longer holding merely because later hindsight eventually became profitable.

  

Persistent neural state should include slow context such as TrendDrive, TrendPersistence, Regime, PainMemory, RewardMemory, Uncertainty, Exposure/Satiety, and prior-session unresolved fear/relief. Fast states such as Panic, Recovery and Reacceleration may decay more quickly. The model should be able to distinguish a fresh morning selloff from the continuation of a multi-day liquidation, and a one-hour bounce from a multi-day bullish continuation.

  

This makes the long-term target a MULTI-HORIZON TRADER, not merely an intraday scalper: it should capture meaningful intraday wrinkles multiple times when they exist, while also being free to keep a good position overnight or for several days when the learned continuation state supports it. Trade frequency is an outcome, not a quota.

UNIFIED FUTURES PLATFORM ROADMAP — 2026-09-15

  

Objective

Build one extensible GridMax strategy platform that can onboard additional TradingView strategies without creating new broker/execution stacks. Existing SOXL production remains protected. All futures portfolios share one durable event model, virtual strategy ledger, activation scheduler, global root aggregation, risk/governor layer, contract authority, execution core, reconciliation engine, and dashboard read model.

  

Current strategy groups

\- SOXL production: existing live system, unchanged by futures integration.

\- MNQ portfolio: Bundle \#2 (CURRENT\_REBASELINED authority), Bundle \#22 (resolved authority), RET\_CON (behavior authority resolved).

\- Core6 intraday portfolio: LC02 MES, LC03 MNQ, LC04 MNQ, LC05 MNQ, LC09 M2K, LC11 MBT.

  

Canonical architecture

TradingView alerts -\> durable raw event journal -\> schema/auth validation -\> deterministic dedup -\> strategy virtual sleeves -\> ACTIVE/PROSPECTIVE activation scheduler -\> portfolio-specific policy/governor -\> GLOBAL execution-root aggregation -\> account-wide risk governor -\> shared ContractAuthority -\> canonical OrderProposal / execution core -\> IBKR -\> callbacks/reconciliation -\> dashboard read model.

  

Critical global invariant

Physical broker ownership is global by execution root, not per portfolio. For each root:

GLOBAL\_BROKER\_TARGET(root) = sum(all ACTIVE GridMax virtual strategy sleeves across all enabled portfolios using that root).

ORDER\_DELTA = GLOBAL\_BROKER\_TARGET - OBSERVED\_IBKR\_POSITION.

This prevents the MNQ swing portfolio and Core6 MNQ strategies from independently controlling the same IBKR MNQ position.

  

Extensibility requirement

Adding a future strategy should normally require configuration/registration plus a versioned signal adapter, not a new execution engine. Each strategy definition should provide: portfolio\_id, strategy\_id, source symbol, execution root, timeframe/session, source/version hash, activation policy, allowed direction, native sleeve size, alert schema/version, contract product family, and portfolio-governor assignment. Generic infrastructure must own persistence, dedup, restart recovery, aggregation, contract resolution, reconciliation, audit history, and dashboard presentation.

  

Execution semantics

\- Keep ACTIVE and PROSPECTIVE separate for every strategy.

\- Preserve strategy intent class (ENTRY/ADD/REDUCE/EXIT/REVERSAL) independently from the final physical BUY/SELL direction.

\- Do not infer strategy ownership from broker net position.

\- Broker-flat can coexist with nonzero virtual gross; track latent un-net exposure and reserve.

\- Strategy-specific timing remains authoritative: slower MNQ strategies may activate next valid RTH 09:30; Core6 5m strategies must be audited from frozen Pine before their exact decision-to-activation semantics are frozen.

  

Contract authority

Use one shared ContractAuthority interface for MES/MNQ/M2K/MBT with product-family-specific lifecycle rules. Strategy sleeves express root-level logical quantity; execution resolves exact localSymbol/expiry/conId/exchange/multiplier/tick size. Contract-level reconciliation is required; old+new contracts are not equivalent merely because root quantity sums match. Roll changes physical contracts, not logical strategy ownership.

  

Roll policy

Equity-index micro roll mechanics are modeled as old-contract exit -\> broker/reconciliation confirmation -\> successor revalidation -\> successor entry, with no temporary double exposure and no blind retry. Roll-trigger policy must be explicitly frozen by product family before live use. Historical volume-crossover/T+1 remains research evidence unless separately promoted to live authority.

  

Rollout roadmap

1\. Unified non-live architecture: integrate MNQ swing + Core6 using one event/sleeve/netting/contract/reconciliation framework.

2\. Strategy parity: verify any webhook-enabled Pine against canonical frozen/rebaselined Pine without changing economics.

3\. Real TradingView SHADOW: real alerts ON, futures broker transmission OFF, futures ARM OFF. Observe end-to-end signal -\> sleeve -\> activation -\> governor -\> global target -\> exact contract -\> proposed order.

4\. Non-executing broker certification: use the same canonical execution path for IBKR WHAT-IF / contract-resolution checks across MES/MNQ/M2K/MBT.

5\. One-micro futures transport canary through the shared execution core after explicit authorization.

6\. Limited futures live: one micro per active Core6 sleeve; MNQ portfolio uses its frozen native sizing/governor; no per-trade human approval once limited-live is enabled.

7\. Scale only through explicit future authority; no automatic scaling.

  

Dashboard product roadmap

The dashboard should be modular and strategy-agnostic rather than hard-coded per strategy.

A. Strategy view: grouped by portfolio; ACTIVE, PROSPECTIVE, next activation, intent type, last signal, freshness, provenance/version, virtual PnL, shadow/live state.

B. Execution-root view: MES/MNQ/M2K/MBT; virtual longs/shorts/gross/net, latent un-net exposure, desired global broker target, observed broker target, delta, authoritative contract/localSymbol/conId, working orders, quote age, reconciliation.

C. Account/portfolio view: SOXL exposure, futures gross/net/latent risk, margin, excess liquidity, daily/cumulative PnL, drawdown, governor decisions, pause/kill state, mode.

D. Next-intent drawer: exactly what GridMax would send, which strategy transition caused it, activation time, contract, quantity, order type, governor/risk decision, and blocker reason.

E. Audit/history drill-down: webhooks, duplicates/rejections, sleeve transitions, activations, governor decisions, contract/roll events, submits/status/fills, reconciliation, manual intervention.

  

UI/UX principles

\- Main page answers five questions immediately: Is the system healthy? What do we own? What does each strategy want next? What exact broker target/order follows? Is any action required?

\- Use compact summary cards and drill-down instead of placing every gate on the main page.

\- Separate CONFIGURED/ENABLED from OPERATIONALLY READY/BLOCKED.

\- Use explicit statuses (ACTIVE, PROSPECTIVE, SHADOW, LIVE, MATCH, BLOCKED, ROLL\_DUE, UNKNOWN) with consistent visual treatment.

\- Clicking a strategy or root should explain provenance and causal path, not only show numbers.

\- Show blocker reasons in plain language; avoid generic “risk failed.”

\- Preserve responsive layout for browser monitoring.

  

Future strategy onboarding checklist

A new strategy should not require new IBKR client ownership or a separate dashboard service.
