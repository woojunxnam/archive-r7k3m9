TEST18 — VWAP ACCEPTANCE / RECLAIM / CROSS-STATE

Status: LOCKED

Core axis: HOW price interacts with VWAP over time, not merely static distance from VWAP.

Do not repeat prior simple VWAP stretch/reclaim or overextension/rejection strategies.



Primary features

1\. VWAP cross count since RTH open.

2\. Consecutive bars above/below VWAP.

3\. Recent dwell fraction above/below VWAP.

4\. Time since last VWAP cross.

5\. VWAP slope over fixed wall-clock windows.

6\. Distance from VWAP / RTH ATR.

7\. Maximum excursion from VWAP before reclaim/failure.

8\. First reclaim versus repeated reclaim.

9\. Failed reclaim: cross VWAP then return to original side within preregistered bars.

10\. Acceptance after reclaim: cross VWAP and remain on the new side for preregistered dwell.

11\. VWAP chop state: many crosses + low displacement.

12\. One-sided VWAP trend state: few crosses + high dwell + aligned VWAP slope.



Mechanism families

A. Clean reclaim + acceptance -\> continuation LONG/SHORT.

B. Failed reclaim -\> continuation toward original side.

C. First VWAP cross after long one-sided dwell -\> transition/mean reversion.

D. Repeated VWAP cross/chop -\> fade excursion back toward VWAP.

E. One-sided VWAP trend + shallow pullback -\> continuation.

F. Large excursion -\> VWAP reclaim -\> continuation only after acceptance.

G. VWAP slope reversal + acceptance shift -\> directional reversal.

H. First reclaim fails, second reclaim succeeds -\> continuation.

Timeframes: 5m primary / 15m validation.

Execution: RTH only; hard flat 15:45 ET.
